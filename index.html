About

Born and raised in Bangkok on December 7, 2000, NackSgro (แน็คสโกร) is a singer-songwriter whose life has been harmonized by music since his earliest years.

His artistic foundation was built on a series of vocal competitions starting in primary school, which later evolved into a decade-long commitment within his secondary school's international music ensemble from Grade 7 through Grade 12 — an immersion in performance and theory that ignited a lasting passion for vocal artistry and instrumental composition.

Now creating independently under DarkroomWhiteroom, NackSgro has entered a new era of creative refinement. While his debut EP in 2021 marked a significant milestone, he has since taken a deliberate, curated approach to his catalog — selectively retiring earlier work so his discography reflects his current artistic vision. New singles and long-awaited projects are set to arrive throughout 2026.
